Sebelum membuat slide, WAJIB melakukan research mendalam.

Cari data terbaru yang tersedia secara publik mengenai:

PT ITSEC Asia Tbk
Bronyx
cybersecurity market Indonesia
enterprise cybersecurity
security testing
penetration testing
automated security testing
SaaS cybersecurity
digital transformation Indonesia

Gunakan sumber:

- official company sources
- official government sources
- OJK
- Bank Indonesia
- BSSN
- Kominfo / Komdigi jika relevan
- IDX jika relevan
- annual reports
- reputable industry reports
- reputable business publications

Jangan menggunakan data tanpa sumber.

==================================================
OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Jawab secara VISUAL:

"How large is the addressable opportunity?"

==================================================
PRIMARY VISUAL
==================================================

Buat:

TAM
SAM
SOM

visualization.

Gunakan:

large numbers
circle chart
donut
funnel
or layered circles

Jika angka tidak tersedia secara langsung:

jelaskan methodology perhitungan.

Jangan membuat angka seolah-olah fakta.

==================================================
MARKET TREND
==================================================

Gunakan:

line chart

untuk menunjukkan trend relevan seperti:

digital adoption
cybersecurity spending
enterprise digitalization
security risk growth

Gunakan data publik yang dapat diverifikasi.

==================================================
KEY MARKET SIGNALS
==================================================

Gunakan 3–5 angka besar.

Contoh:

X
%
growth

X
companies

X
market size

X
digital users

Tetapi hanya gunakan angka yang benar-benar memiliki sumber.

==================================================
SOURCE SYSTEM
==================================================

Setiap visual data harus mempunyai:

Source
Year
Data context

Jangan membuat bibliography panjang di tengah slide.

Gunakan source note kecil di bagian bawah.

==================================================
DESIGN
==================================================

Angka harus jauh lebih dominan daripada paragraph.

Jangan menjelaskan data dengan teks panjang.

Visual harus langsung menunjukkan:

MARKET SIZE
GROWTH
OPPORTUNITY

==================================================
IMPORTANT
==================================================

Jika data Indonesia tidak cukup kuat:

gunakan proxy yang jelas.
