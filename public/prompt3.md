WAJIB melakukan research.

Tujuannya adalah membuat:

INDONESIA CYBERSECURITY TARGET MARKET MAP

==================================================
DATA RESEARCH
==================================================

Cari dan kumpulkan perusahaan publik / organisasi besar di Indonesia yang secara logis memiliki kebutuhan tinggi terhadap cybersecurity.

Kelompokkan minimal:

1. GOVERNMENT / PUBLIC SECTOR

2. BANKING

3. FINTECH

4. INSURANCE

5. E-COMMERCE

6. TELECOMMUNICATION

7. TECHNOLOGY

8. ENERGY

9. MINING

10. MANUFACTURING

11. LOGISTICS

12. HEALTHCARE

13. TRAVEL / HOSPITALITY

14. DIGITAL SERVICES

15. OTHER ENTERPRISE

==================================================
IMPORTANT FACT-CHECK
==================================================

JANGAN menyatakan perusahaan tersebut sebagai:

customer ITSEC
existing lead
active opportunity
prospect ITSEC

kecuali ada sumber publik yang benar-benar mendukung.

Gunakan istilah:

TARGET ACCOUNT
POTENTIAL ACCOUNT
MARKET TARGET
PROSPECT PROFILE

jika status sebenarnya tidak diketahui.

==================================================
GEOGRAPHIC MAP
==================================================

Buat peta Indonesia yang interactive.

Tampilkan target accounts sebagai nodes.

Contoh:

Jakarta
Bandung
Surabaya
Medan
Makassar
Bali
dan kota relevan lainnya.

Gunakan:

RED = highest priority
GRAY = lower priority
WHITE = neutral

Ukuran node menunjukkan:

estimated strategic importance

Jangan menggunakan data palsu.

==================================================
ACCOUNT BUBBLE
==================================================

Ketika node dipilih:

tampilkan:

Company
Industry
Location
Digital Scale
Potential Cybersecurity Need
Why Relevant
Potential Buyer

Jika data tersedia:

Company Size
Public / Private
Listed / Unlisted

==================================================
SECTOR FILTER
==================================================

Buat filter:

Government
Banking
Fintech
E-commerce
Telco
Technology
Energy
Mining
Manufacturing
Healthcare
Others

Ketika filter berubah:

map berubah secara animated.

==================================================
ACCOUNT PRIORITY
==================================================

Gunakan scoring framework:

Digital Complexity
+
Security Exposure
+
Regulatory Pressure
+
Business Scale
+
Potential Fit

Tampilkan sebagai:

circle size
position
heatmap
score

Jangan hanya memakai badge.

==================================================
VISUAL PRIORITY
==================================================

MAP harus menjadi visual utama.

Teks hanya:

WHY THIS SECTOR
WHY THIS ACCOUNT
NEXT ACTION

==================================================
FINAL OUTPUT
==================================================

Audiens harus dapat melihat:

"Indonesia is not one market.
It is a portfolio of account opportunities."
